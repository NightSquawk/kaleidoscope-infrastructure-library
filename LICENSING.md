# Licensing boundaries

Catalog data and original artwork are dedicated under CC0-1.0. Tooling, schemas, documentation and example application code are Apache-2.0. Full texts are in LICENSES/.

asset-manifest.json assigns an exact license, origin, source, revision, copyright statement, modification note and SHA-256 to every distributed file except the manifest itself. The manifest is CC0-1.0. Folder names help navigation; the manifest is authoritative per file.

Imported NetBox files are preserved byte-for-byte in catalog/upstream/netbox/CC0-1.0/. Our additions live separately. Source and commit are recorded in provenance.json. No upstream elevation images or vendor logos are included.

Third-party artwork must live under artwork/third-party/<source>/<SPDX-license>/ with source URL, rights evidence, attribution and required notices. Only CC0-1.0 and Apache-2.0 are allowed by this starter's release gate. Adding another license requires reviewing its obligations, extending policy and including its license and notices. This allowlist is a project policy, not a legal compatibility determination.

Unknown licenses and missing provenance block release. Source availability alone is not permission. Contributors must own their contributions or document permission. Do not include customer assets, IP addresses, credentials or production telemetry. CC0 does not waive third-party trademark rights or imply vendor endorsement.

Generated releases include the manifest, license texts and notices. A model's license never overrides the license of referenced artwork.
